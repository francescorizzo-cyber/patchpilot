FAQ

Is there an Android app for the Zoom B1 FOUR?

Yes: PatchPilot for Android recognises the Zoom B1 FOUR as soon as you plug in the USB-C / OTG cable, with the AI sound coach, the coach without AI and tone from a song. It is coming soon on Google Play; today PatchPilot runs on Windows, macOS and Linux.

Can I back up my Zoom B1 FOUR patches?

Yes. At the first connection PatchPilot reads all the patches and saves a backup. The backup is a file with date and time that you can export and share.

Is PatchPilot made by the pedal maker?

No. PatchPilot is an independent project, not affiliated with Zoom, Boss/Roland, Valeton, NUX or Line 6.
