All features

Everything PatchPilot does today, in the version you download. Nothing is ever written to the pedal.

Look inside your pedal

🔌

Detects the pedal by itself. Plug in the USB cable and PatchPilot tells you the model and firmware version. For models that do not identify themselves you pick it from a list.

📋

Full patch list. Every patch on the pedal with its name, at a glance, with the selected one highlighted.

🎛️

Effects and parameters editor. Every slot with its effect, on or off, and all its parameters. Knob or fader view: turn them to try things out — it all stays in the program.

🏷️

Real effect and parameter names. Import your own .ZD2 files and see effect and knob names instead of numbers.

🎸

Needle tuner. The pedal's tuner, big on the screen, with note and cents: readable from across the room.

🖼️

A pedal-shaped screen. The main page draws your model: display, knobs and switches are clickable.

Keep your sounds safe

💾

Backup of every patch. Save all the pedal's patches to a file and reopen them any time, even with no pedal connected.

📄

Sound sheets. A readable sheet for each patch — effects, on/off, parameters — to print, save or share.

🔎

Search by effect. “Where did I use that delay?” Find every patch that uses an effect.

⚖️

Compare two patches. Put two patches side by side and see only the differences.

On stage

🎶

Setlist. Order your songs and pick the patch for each, one per pedal if you use several. With capo and notes.

⏭️

Song bar. Change song with the mouse wheel, arrow keys, space bar or a USB page-turner pedal, and search a song by name.

🌙

Stage mode. A dark screen with big letters: which patch to select now and what comes next.

🥁

Songs that follow the tempo. Split a song into sections with their bars: count-in, beats on screen and a “GET READY” warning before every change. Also shows delay times.

🌊

Gradual transitions. The sound changes little by little, like a virtual expression pedal, over the seconds or bars you choose.

Volume and levels

🔊

Every patch at the same volume. Play the same riff with each patch: PatchPilot measures loudness as the ear hears it (LUFS) and tells you the patch level to set.

📈

Audio level meter. If your pedal is also a USB audio interface, see the signal level. Sound is only measured, never recorded.

🎚️

MIDI faders. Four sliders sending standard Control Change (volume, expression…) to a generic MIDI pedal.

Other pedals and community

🎹

Any MIDI pedal. With a pedal of another brand the computer sends the patch change for every song in the setlist.

📚

Community patch library. Patches shared by PatchPilot users, free for everyone (CC0), searchable by song, artist, genre and pedal.

🙋

Tester page. Your pedal is not listed? A guided, listen-only recording helps us add it.

🧾

Log. Everything the program and the pedal say to each other, to save and send if something goes wrong.

Your pedal is safe

PatchPilot only reads. It never writes, saves or deletes anything in your pedal's memory: those commands are blocked in the code. Backups, sound sheets and setlists are files on your phone or computer. Changing patch from the app and editing parameters will come only after tests on real pedals.

Frequently asked questions

Is there an Android app for the Zoom MS-50G+, MS-60B+ or MS-70CDR+?

Zoom's Handy Guitar Lab apps for the MultiStomp+ pedals are for iPhone. PatchPilot is a program for Windows, macOS and Linux, with the Android app coming soon on Google Play, that reads patches, effects and parameters from these pedals over USB. It is a beta.

Can PatchPilot edit or save patches?

Not yet. The beta only reads from the pedal. Editing parameters and changing patch will be added step by step after tests on real pedals.

Which cable do I need?

A USB-C to USB-C cable, or an OTG adapter with your pedal's USB cable. MultiStomp+ pedals usually also need their power supply, because a phone may not power them.

Does it work on a computer?

Yes: Windows 10/11, macOS (Apple Silicon and Intel) and Linux, with the same functions. Java is included in the download. Close Guitar Lab first: the pedal's MIDI port works with one program at a time.

How much does it cost?

Free during the beta. The full version is €4.99, paid once per device, by card (Stripe) or PayPal; you get a personal unlock code.

Can PatchPilot make all my patches the same volume?

Yes, from version 0.9, on Windows, macOS and Linux: you play the same riff with each patch, PatchPilot measures the perceived loudness (LUFS) and tells you the patch level that makes them even. The sound is only measured, never recorded.

Is there a library of free Zoom patches?

Yes: the PatchPilot community library, a public repository of patches shared by users under CC0, searchable by song, artist, genre and pedal.

Is PatchPilot made by Zoom?

No. PatchPilot is an independent project, not affiliated with Zoom Corporation.

Is PatchPilot an alternative to Guitar Lab?

For looking inside the pedal, backing up, building a setlist and playing live, yes — and it also runs on Linux. It does not write patches to the pedal yet: for that you still need Zoom's own software.

Does PatchPilot use AI?

From version 0.9.1: “Describe the sound” suggests which knobs to turn and “Explain this patch” explains every effect. Suggestions stay in the program; only your description and the patch values go to the service, never audio.

Does it work with pedals from other brands?

Yes, over standard MIDI: with any MIDI pedal the computer sends the patch change for every song. From 0.9.1 there are ready-made profiles for Line 6 HX Stomp and Valeton GP-200.
